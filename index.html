A quality-first mindset.
A people-first approach.

Noel CanlasQA ENGINEER → FRONT-END DEVELOPER

Good software isn’t just about what it can do.

Working in software QA has taught me to look beyond the happy path. I collaborate with developers and product managers to clarify requirements, explore edge cases, and make the user experience more dependable.

As I complete my computer science degree, I’m bringing that perspective into front-end development. I’m especially interested in turning product requirements into clear interfaces and using testing to support thoughtful design decisions.
